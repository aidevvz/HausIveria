Where four walls become a home.

Your English-speaking real estate agent in Vienna and Lower Austria. Personal advice on buying, selling and valuing property, in English, German and Russian.

Buying or selling a home in a foreign country is daunting when you don‘t speak the language or know the rules. Iveria Immobilien is a personal, independent real estate agency in Vienna that works closely with international clients. We advise you in your own language, explain every step, and look after one client at a time rather than running you through a call centre.

What we do

We help owners sell, buyers find the right home, and everyone understand what a property is really worth. Whether you are relocating to Vienna, investing from abroad, or selling an apartment you have owned for years, you get one dedicated contact from the first call to the handover of the keys.

  • Buy a home in Vienna: from the search to the signing, with the local knowledge and the language you need.
  • Sell your property: honest pricing, careful marketing, a calm and professional process.
  • Property valuation: a free, no-obligation assessment of what your home is worth today.

Why Iveria

We are based in Vienna‘s 12th district, right on the edge of the 23rd and the Lower Austrian border, and we know these neighbourhoods first-hand, not from a spreadsheet. With around ten years in the Austrian market and advice in English, German and Russian, we are the natural choice for international owners and buyers who want a broker who actually understands their situation.

The first step is always an honest valuation or a short conversation. Get in touch and we‘ll take it from there.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

01Do you work with international and English-speaking clients?

Yes. A large part of our work is with international buyers, sellers and tenants in Vienna. We advise fluently in English, German and Russian, and also speak Georgian.

02Which areas do you cover?

Our focus is the west and south-west of Vienna and the neighbouring part of Lower Austria, the area around our office in the 12th district. For other locations, just ask.

03Is the first consultation free?

Yes. The first conversation and an honest initial assessment of your property are free and without obligation.
