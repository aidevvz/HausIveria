For owners

Sell your property with care.

Sell your apartment or house in Vienna with a personal, English-speaking agent. Honest pricing, careful marketing and a calm, professional process.

Selling a home is a big decision, and often an emotional one. Our job is to make it calm, clear and well-run, and to get you the right price without the usual sales pressure. You deal with one person throughout, who handles the viewings personally and keeps you informed at every stage.

How we sell

We begin with an honest valuation based on the real market, not an inflated figure to win the mandate. Then we prepare the property properly: good photography, a clear and complete set of documents, and marketing aimed at the buyers who are genuinely right for your home.

We deliberately take on fewer mandates so that each one gets real attention. For international owners, we handle the whole process in English and keep the communication simple, wherever you are based.

From valuation to handover

Once we agree on the strategy and price, we market the property, qualify the interested buyers, and guide the sale through the offer and the purchase contract, which is drawn up and settled in escrow by a notary or lawyer. We stay with you through to the handover of the keys.

Costs and taxes

The agent‘s commission on a sale is capped at 3 percent plus VAT per party and is only due if the sale succeeds. On the gain, real estate capital gains tax of 30 percent may apply unless an exemption fits your case. We flag what is relevant for you early, so there are no surprises at the end.

The first step is simple and free: request a valuation or get in touch for a short, no-obligation conversation.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

01What is the agent's commission when selling?

On a sale the legal maximum is 3 percent of the price plus 20 percent VAT, per party. The rate is negotiable and only due on success, once a sale is actually concluded and agreed in writing in advance.

02What taxes apply when I sell?

A gain on the sale is generally subject to real estate capital gains tax (Immobilienertragsteuer) of 30 percent, unless an exemption applies, most importantly the main-residence exemption for a home you lived in. The notary calculates the exact amount.

03Do I need an energy certificate to sell?

Yes. An energy certificate (Energieausweis) is mandatory and its key values must already appear in the listing, so it is best to arrange it early, before marketing starts.
