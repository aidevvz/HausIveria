Buying or selling a home in a foreign country is daunting when you don't speak the language or know the rules. Iveria Immobilien is a personal, independent real estate agency in Vienna that works closely with international clients. We advise you in your own language, explain every step, and look after one client at a time rather than running you through a call centre.

## What we do

We help owners sell, buyers find the right home, and everyone understand what a property is really worth. Whether you are relocating to Vienna, investing from abroad, or selling an apartment you have owned for years, you get one dedicated contact from the first call to the handover of the keys.

- [Buy a home in Vienna](/en/buy.html): from the search to the signing, with the local knowledge and the language you need.
- [Sell your property](/en/sell.html): honest pricing, careful marketing, a calm and professional process.
- [Property valuation](/en/valuation.html): a free, no-obligation assessment of what your home is worth today.

## Why Iveria

We are based in Vienna's 12th district, right on the edge of the 23rd and the Lower Austrian border, and we know these neighbourhoods first-hand, not from a spreadsheet. With around ten years in the Austrian market and advice in English, German and Russian, we are the natural choice for international owners and buyers who want a broker who actually understands their situation.

The first step is always an honest [valuation](/en/valuation.html) or a short conversation. [Get in touch](/en/contact.html) and we'll take it from there.
