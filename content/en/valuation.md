Before any decision about selling, it helps to know what your property is actually worth today. We give you an honest, well-founded valuation, based on the real market in your area, and we tell you how we arrived at the figure rather than just handing you a number.

## How we value your property

A sound valuation combines two things: what comparable properties have really sold for, and the specifics of yours. Condition, floor plan, floor, outlook, and above all the exact location within the district can move the figure substantially. That is why an online calculator can only ever be a rough guide, and a real valuation needs a short look at the property itself.

For houses in particular, there is no general price per square metre: the value depends heavily on the individual property and its micro-location.

## Honest, not inflated

Some agents quote a high figure simply to win the mandate, then push you to lower the price weeks later. We don't. You get a realistic range from the start, so your property is priced right and sells to the right buyer, instead of sitting on the market and losing its appeal.

The valuation is free and without obligation. [Get in touch](/en/contact.html) and we'll arrange it. If you decide to go ahead, read how we handle the [sale](/en/sell.html).
